CastBridge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

CastBridge streams the audio playing on your Android phone to AirPlay 2 speakers on your local network. This page explains exactly what the app does with your data — in short: nothing leaves your device or your local Wi-Fi.

What the app handles

  • Audio. With your explicit consent (Android's capture prompt, shown every time you start casting), CastBridge captures the audio playing on your phone and streams it to the speaker you select. Audio is processed in memory, on your device and your local network only. It is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to us or any third party.
  • Local network information. To find speakers, the app discovers AirPlay devices advertised on your Wi-Fi network (mDNS/Bonjour). This information stays on your device.
  • Settings. The speaker and preferences you choose are stored locally on your device only.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No data collection, no uploads, no sharing. The app has no servers.

Permissions and why

  • MediaProjection / record audio — to capture what is playing so it can be streamed to your speaker. You confirm this every time you start casting.
  • Local network access — to discover and connect to AirPlay speakers.
  • Notifications — to show casting status and volume controls.
  • Foreground service — to keep the stream running while the app is in the background.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
